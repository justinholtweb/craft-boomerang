The state machine

States are
yours to name

The plugin reads each state's kind, not its label.

  • Seven states and eight reasons seeded on install
  • Empty transition list means anywhere: the escape hatch
  • Append-only history: who moved it, when, and why
  • They travel between environments with boomerang/config
A state can
Email  ·  Restock  ·  Resolve  ·  Label
Four switches per state, carried out on arrival, in that order.
Kinds, not labels
requested · approved · awaiting · received
Plus resolved, rejected and cancelled. Call them what your desk calls them; the plugin still knows what they mean.
A refused refund
aborts the transition
Side effects run before the state is written, so an RMA is never marked resolved with no money having moved.

Store credit

Credit that
spends

A payment method, not a discount that lies about the subtotal.

  • "Store credit — $40.00", beside the card at checkout
  • $40 against a $95 order pays $40; the card pays $55
  • Issued in lots, spent oldest-expiry first, under a lock
  • A balance is a sum over lots, never a stored number

Resolutions

Money, credit
or goods

What the customer gets back, and where the stock goes while they get it.

  • Refunds run through Commerce's own gateway transactions
  • An adjustment, so fulfilled stock never goes negative
  • Idempotent per item, however often it is retried
  • Snapshot prices, so a late refund is the agreed one
refund
Back to the card
A partial success is written before it throws, so the ledger is never behind the gateway.
credit
Into the wallet
With creditBonusPercent, a $50 refund becomes $55 of credit — the lever that makes customers pick it.
exchange
Out as new goods
A replacement order carrying a credit for what came back, so the customer pays only the difference.
restock
Onto the right shelf
Resellable to available, damaged to damaged, anything questionable to quality control.

Operations

Runnable
from a cron

The desk's whole week, in commands that fail loudly.

  • Expiry is enforced in the balance, not only by the cron
  • Uncaptured authorizations go back to their lots
  • A label preview prints the request without buying it
  • States and reasons export and import as one JSON file

! RMA-00037   In transit    16 days

$ php craft boomerang/credit/balance kim@example.com
